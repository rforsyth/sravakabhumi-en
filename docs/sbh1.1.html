Gotrabhūmi: The Stage of Lineage

Śrāvakabhūmi · Sanskrit (Shukla 1973) ↔ AI-assisted English
← All topicsView English only →

The stage of lineage (gotra): what makes a person fit for the disciple's path. The Sanskrit begins at 1.2, in the middle of the list of the five factors of endowment-from-self; the opening of the stage survives in Xuanzang's Chinese translation (T1579 juan 21). What survives gives the conditions under which the lineage is perfected (1.2–10.24), the marks of a person established in the lineage, stated as the opposites of six marks of one who is not (11.1–13.5), and the kinds of persons established in the lineage (14.1–26.1). The text breaks off at 26.1, at the definition of the person who is near.

What is the arising of Buddhas?
sbh1.1:1.28
yathāpīhaikatyaḥ sarvasarvasatve ttve ṣu kalyāṇaṁ hitādhyāśayamutpādya prabhūtairduṣkarasahasrairmahatā ca puṇyajñānasambhāreṇa ātmabhāvapratilambhe bodhimaṇḍe niṣadya, pañcanivaraṇāni prahāya, caturṣu smṛtyupasthāneṣu sūpasthitacittaḥ, saptatriṁśad bodhipakṣyān dharmān bhāvayitvā a nuttarāṁ samyaksaṁbodhimabhisaṁbudhyate |
When someone, having generated benevolent intention toward all beings, attains [a final] individual existence through thousands of difficult deeds and a great accumulation of merit and knowledge — and seated on the seat of awakening, having abandoned the five hindrances, with mind well-established in the four foundations of mindfulness, having cultivated the thirty-seven dharmas conducive to awakening, he awakens to supreme, perfect awakening.

This is called the arising of Buddhas.
sbh1.1:1.30
atītānāgatapratyutpanneṣvadhvasu evamevā kleśa ā yā ta evaṁ buddhā bhagavanto loka utpadya tasyaiva ca śrāvakalokānukampāmupādāya catvāryāryasatyānyārabhya duḥkha sadbhirayañca dharmo niryāto, deśitaḥ, praśasto buddhaiśca buddhaśrāvakaiścā yamucyate buddhānāmutpādaḥ |
In past, future, and present times, just so [as described]. [Conjectural: What is the teaching of the true dharma? When] Buddhas, blessed ones, having arisen in the world, out of compassion for the world of disciples, [Conjectural: teach] with reference to the four noble truths beginning with suffering; and this dharma, by which good persons have gone forth to deliverance, is taught and praised by the Buddhas and by the disciples of the Buddhas — this is called [Conjectural: the teaching of the true dharma].
Printed evam evā [ ] [kleśa ā yā] ta evaṁ, illegible and broken (Shukla p. 7 nn. 4–5), and the close [ayam ucyate buddhānām utpādaḥ] is Shukla's supplement; the segment defines saddharmadeśanā and the sense follows Xuanzang 云何名為說正法教…名正法教 (T1579 juan 21).

What is the establishment of the taught dharmas?

What is the further transmission of the established dharmas?

He, through not having begun application toward nirvāṇa, is far by remoteness in application, [Conjectural: not] by remoteness in time — this is called the person who is far.
