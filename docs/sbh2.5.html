Analysis of Elements

Śrāvakabhūmi · Sanskrit (Shukla 1973) ↔ AI-assisted English
← All topicsView English only →

The conduct-purifying object for the person of conceit-conduct (¶8). The six elements are listed at 1.2–1.3 and taken one by one: earth (¶2), water (¶3), fire (¶4), and wind (¶5), each divided into inward, what belongs to this body, and outward; then space (¶6) and consciousness (¶7), without that division. Attending to this analysis, the conceit-conduct person dispels the perception of the body as a unified lump and purifies the mind from conceit (8.1–8.5).
