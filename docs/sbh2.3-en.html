Lovingkindness

The conduct-purifying object for the person of ill-will-conduct (5.1–5.2). ¶1 defines lovingkindness: its object is the three sides, friend, enemy, and neutral; the apprehending [mind] is the benevolent intention and the resolve to bring mild, middling, or strong happiness, grounded in the concentrated plane (1.2–1.11). ¶2–3 explain the formula 'with mind imbued with lovingkindness …' phrase by phrase, and ¶4 assigns the beings who are neither suffering nor happy to lovingkindness, the suffering to compassion, and the happy to sympathetic joy (4.4–4.8). The practice on this object is set out in sbh3.2 (¶18–25 and ¶54).

What is lovingkindness?

Toward the friend-side, the enemy-side, or the neutral-side, having established the benevolent intention, [it is] the resolve to bring mild, middling, or strong happiness — [a resolve] grounded in the concentrated plane. The friend-side, enemy-side, and neutral-side — these are the object. The benevolent intention and the resolve to bring happiness, grounded in the concentrated plane — this is the apprehending [mind]. The object and the apprehending [mind], combined into one — this is called lovingkindness.

When it first says 'with mind imbued with lovingkindness,' by this the benevolent intention toward the three sides — friend, enemy, neutral — is expressed. When it further says: 'without enmity, [Conjectural: without opposition], without harming' — by this, the threefold characteristic of that benevolent intention is expressed. Of these: by non-enmity, the benevolent intention [is characterized]. This non-enmity is expressed by two further terms: [Conjectural: non-opposition] and non-harming. [Conjectural: Non-opposition]: in the sense of standing in a non-adversarial state. Non-harming: in the sense of [Conjectural: not wishing] harm.

When it further says: 'vast, exalted, [Conjectural: immeasurable]' — by this, the bringing of mild, middling, or strong happiness is expressed: happiness of the desire-realm, or of the first-and-second-absorption planes, or of the third-absorption plane. When it further says: 'having resolved, [Conjectural: having pervaded], he attains and abides' — by this, the resolve to bring happiness, grounded in the concentrated plane, is expressed.

This bringing of happiness, encompassed by the benevolent intention, characterized by resolve, accompanied by attention — should be understood as directed toward those who are neither-suffering-nor-happy, desiring happiness, on the friend-side, enemy-side, or neutral-side. Whereas for the one who is suffering, or the [Conjectural: happy] one, on the friend-side, the enemy-side, or the neutral-side: the suffering one is the object of compassion; the happy one is the object of sympathetic joy. This is called lovingkindness.

Here, the ill-will-conduct person, cultivating lovingkindness, attenuates the ill-will toward beings. He purifies the mind from ill-will.
