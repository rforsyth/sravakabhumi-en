# Translator's notes: sbh3.1 (Navākārā cittasthitiḥ — The Nine Stages of Mental Abiding)

The *śamatha* side of one-pointedness (1.1–1.2). The nine stages are named at 2.2–2.9, placing, placing continuously, placing again, placing closely, taming, pacifying, fully pacifying, making single-pointed, and concentrating, and defined in turn at ¶3–10; the definition of placing closely is lost in the lacuna at 5.3. ¶11–12 assign the nine to the six powers that accomplish them, and ¶13–14 to the four attentions, forceful, interrupted, uninterrupted, and effortless. 15.1–15.3 add that for one who turns from this inward calm to *vipaśyanā*, the same four attentions belong to the *vipaśyanā* side.
