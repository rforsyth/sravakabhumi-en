# Translator's notes: sbh3.1 (Navākārā cittasthitiḥ — The Nine Stages of Mental Abiding)

The classical formulation of the nine *cittasthiti* (mental abiding) stages: *sthāpayati* (places), *saṁsthāpayati* (places continuously), *avasthāpayati* (places again), *upasthāpayati* (places closely), *damayati* (tames), *śamayati* (pacifies), *vyupaśamayati* (fully pacifies), *ekotīkaroti* (makes single-pointed), *samādhatte* (concentrates). Together with the six powers (*ṣaḍ-vidha-bala*) that traverse them, the four modes of mental engagement (*catur-vidha manaskāra*), and the bridge to *vipaśyanā*, this is the canonical śamatha map adopted across the Buddhist meditation tradition (cited by Asaṅga's brother Vasubandhu, by the Tibetan stages-of-the-path literature, and through modern lineages).
