# Translator's notes: sbh4.2 (Higher Dhyānas, Formless Attainments, and Higher Knowledges)

The mundane path continued from sbh4.1 ¶23–24 beyond the first *dhyāna*. ¶1 states the twofold gross characteristic of every lower plane up to the sphere of nothingness: more suffering and less calm, and a shorter life-span. ¶2–6 explain the formulas for the four *dhyānas* phrase by phrase ('secluded from sense-pleasures …', 1.7–2.11; the second, ¶3; the third, ¶4–5; the fourth, ¶6), and ¶7–10 those of the four formless attainments. ¶12 treats the two attainments without mind, non-perception and cessation; ¶13 the bringing forth of the five higher knowledges with *dhyāna* as support; ¶14–15 the twelve perceptions cultivated for them; ¶16 the difference between noble and ignoble supernatural power. Their fruits follow at sbh4.3 ¶1.
