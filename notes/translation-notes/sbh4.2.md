# Translator's notes: sbh4.2 (Higher Jhāna and Formless Transitions)

The seven *manaskāras* of [sbh4.1](sbh4.1.html) generalized through the higher transitions, governed by the *audārika-lakṣaṇa* (gross-characteristic) pattern: each higher attainment is reached by recognizing the grossness of the lower and the peace of the higher. Seven transitions: 1st→2nd, 2nd→3rd, 3rd→4th *jhāna*; 4th *jhāna*→*ākāśānantyāyatana*, *ākāśa*→*vijñāna*, *vijñāna*→*ākiñcanya*, *ākiñcanya*→*naivasaṁjñā-nāsaṁjñāyatana*. Plus the two non-conscious attainments (*asaṁjñā-* and *nirodha-samāpatti*) and the five *abhijñā* (super-knowledges) with their twelve perceptions.


The closing five-*abhijñā* material at ¶11 describes how pure *dhyāna*, concentrated-plane attention, and repeated cultivation condition the arising of the five mundane higher knowledges. The sixth higher knowledge, destruction of outflows, belongs to the supramundane path and is not included here.
