# Translator's notes: sbh4.1 (Sapta-manaskāra — The Seven Attentions)

Yogasthāna IV opens with the practitioner who has obtained attention choosing between the mundane and the supramundane course (¶1); ¶2 names the four persons who take the mundane path only, ¶3 its scope. The mundane path to the first *dhyāna* is then set out as seven attentions, named at 4.5–4.7. In ¶5–13 the characteristic-knowing attention investigates sense-pleasures by the six investigation-subjects (gross meaning, entity, specific and general characteristic, side, time, reasoning) and finds the first *dhyāna* calm by contrast; ¶14–20 take the remaining six through to the attainment of the first *dhyāna*; ¶21–22 say what each accomplishes; ¶23–24 apply the seven to the higher *dhyānas* and formless attainments, continued in sbh4.2. sbh4.3:3.1 applies the same seven to the supramundane path.
