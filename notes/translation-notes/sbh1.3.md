# Translator's notes: sbh1.3 (Naiṣkramyabhūmi)

The stage of renunciation (*naiṣkramya*), the longest section of the first yogasthāna. The text defines it as going to dispassion by the worldly path, through the dhyānas and the formless attainments (1.6–14), and by the supramundane path, through knowledge of the four truths and cultivation of the path (1.15–22), together with the requisites (*saṁbhāra*) for both (1.5). The body of the section (2.1–80.104) treats the requisites one by one, from restraint of virtue to the ornament of the recluse; summary verses and the colophon of the first yogasthāna close it (81.1–86.1).

## Structure

- 1.1–1.22. The definition.
- 2.1–2.5. The requisites listed; the first three (endowment-from-self, endowment-from-others, wholesome dharma-desire) are referred back to the gotrabhūmi (2.4–5; sbh1.1:10.20–21).
- 2.6–31.3. Restraint of virtue, closing with a summary verse (30.1–31.3).
- 32.1–35.66. Restraint of the senses (the list at 2.3 omits it; see the note there).
- 36.1–41.1. Knowing the right measure in food.
- 42.1–42.229. Application to wakefulness in the early and late watches of the night.
- 43.1–55.1. Dwelling with full awareness (to 47.66, then the sequence of its practice from 48.1) and association with good friends, whose eight factors are summed up at 54.51–53 (summary meaning 54.55–55.1). The manuscript breaks off at 52.2 and resumes within the second factor; the lost passage survives in Xuanzang.
- 56.1–63.39. Hearing-and-reflection on the true dharma: the true dharma as the twelve genres of the word (56.5–62.17), assigned to the three baskets at 62.4–14; hearing and reflection on it (62.18–63.39), including the four reasonings (62.43–63.37).
- 64.1–65.90. Non-obstacle, defined as the absence of the inward and outward obstacles the text lists (64.2–6, 65.89–90).
- 66.1–72.32. Generosity.
- 73.1–80.104. The ornament of the recluse: the qualities listed at 73.2–9 and each treated in turn, among them the ascetic qualities (*dhutaguṇa*), thirteen named at 74.53–59 and counted as twelve at 74.60, paired at 80.1–16 with the lusts they abandon.
- 81.1–86.1. Summary verses: the requisites (82.1–83.2) and the qualities of the ornament (84.1–85.1); colophon of the first yogasthāna (86.1).
