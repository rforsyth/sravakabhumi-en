# Translator's notes: sbh2.2 (Aśubhā — Foulness)

The first of the five *carita-viśodhana ālambana* — meditation objects matched to specific temperaments — corresponding to the *rāga-carita* (lust-temperament) practitioner. The Śrāvakabhūmi enumerates six kinds of foulness: *pratyaśubhatā* (foulness of becoming), *duḥkhāśubhatā* (foulness of suffering), *vāsanāśubhatā*, *gantv-āśubhatā*, *prāptyāśubhatā*, and *sākalyāśubhatā* — covering the foulness of inner aggregates, outer cemetery contemplations, and the five kinds of lust they each address.
