# Translator's notes: sbh2.7 (Kauśalya-ālambana — The Five Skill-Objects)

The last dedicated *ālambana* chapter in Yogasthāna II. *Kauśalya* is third in the canonical four-fold order (*vyāpī*, *carita-viśodhana*, *kauśalya*, *kleśa-viśodhana*); the fourth, *kleśa-viśodhana*, has no separate chapter — it is treated within [sbh2.0](sbh2.0.html) itself, through the four-noble-truths discussion that grounds the supramundane path. The *kauśalya-ālambana* (skill-object) names five domains in which the practitioner cultivates skill: *skandha-kauśalya* (skill in the aggregates), *dhātu-kauśalya* (in the elements), *āyatana-kauśalya* (in the spheres), *pratītyasamutpāda-kauśalya* (in dependent origination), and *sthānāsthāna-kauśalya* (in the possible-and-impossible). Each is treated through both *svalakṣaṇa* (specific characteristic) and *sāmānya-lakṣaṇa* (general characteristic) framing.
