# Translator's notes: sbh1.2 (Avatārabhūmi)

The stage of descent (*avatāra*): the entry of a person of the lineage into the path. The Sanskrit begins at 1.1, part-way into the stage; the lost opening, which treated the own-nature of descent and began its classification (Shukla p. 25 n. 1), survives in Xuanzang's Chinese translation (T1579 juan 21). The text defines six persons, from the merely fit to the one arrived at the ultimate (1.1–3.4); states that the time to final nirvāṇa is not fixed (4.1–13); gives eight marks of the descended person (4.14–10.4); and classifies descended persons (11.1–16.3), closing with a summary, the verse, and a colophon (16.4–19.1).

## Structure

- 1.1–1.27. Six persons, listed at 1.1–3 and defined at 1.4–27: the merely fit; one of mild, middle, and strong wholesome roots (the strong-roots definition is lost, 1.15); one applied to the ultimate; one arrived at the ultimate.
- 2.1–3.4. One person passes through the six in order: descent with the mild roots, maturing with the middle, the strong roots, then the three lower fruits (applied to the ultimate), then arhatship (arrived at the ultimate) (2.1–13); the six show the whole course of the disciple from beginning to end (3.1–4).
- 4.1–4.13. The time to final nirvāṇa is not fixed: at the swiftest three births, at most seven existences; "the delineation of descent" (4.13).
- 4.14–10.4. Eight marks of the descended person (4.15–26, 5.1–6.3, 6.4, 6.5–6, 7, 8, 9, 10).
- 11.1–13.9. The marks are many and only a portion is stated; they are mild, middle, or strong with the person's roots (13.1–4); they are inferential, and only Buddhas and perfected disciples see lineage and descent directly (13.5–9).
- 14.1–16.3. Descended persons: descended only, matured, gone out (14.1–15.2); the other categories as in the gotrabhūmi (16.1–3).
- 16.4–19.1. Summary of the four topics (16.4–5), the verse (17.1–18.2), colophon (19.1).

"Gone out" (14.3–15.2) is not "gone forth" (4.17–22): the text refers to the gotrabhūmi for the division (15.2), where one gone out is a gainer of the noble path and its fruit (sbh1.1:18.2).
