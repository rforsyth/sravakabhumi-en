# Translator's notes: sbh4.3 (Closing Material — Rebirth, Lokottara Path, Arhatship)

¶1–2 close the mundane path: rebirth among the gods of the *dhyāna* and formless planes according to the degree of cultivation (1.2–1.11), and the signs of one without lust (1.12–2.3). The supramundane path begins at 3.1: the same seven attentions, now directed to the four noble truths under sixteen aspects (3.4–3.10). The characteristic-knowing attention occupies ¶4–23, most of it the examination of the truth of suffering, beginning with impermanence (¶5–22). ¶24–29 carry the remaining attentions through direct realization and the fruits of stream-entry, once-returning, and non-returning (¶28), the nature, action, and eleven kinds of cultivation (28.47–29.16), the diamond-like concentration, and arhatship (29.17–29.65). A summary verse (¶30) and the colophons of the fourth yogasthāna and of the Śrāvakabhūmi close the text.
