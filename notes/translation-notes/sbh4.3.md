# Translator's notes: sbh4.3 (Closing Material — Rebirth, Lokottara Path, Arhatship)

The closing chunk of Yogasthāna IV — covering the *lokottara-mārga* (supramundane path) proper, complementing the *laukika-mārga* (mundane path) treated in [sbh4.1](sbh4.1.html) and [sbh4.2](sbh4.2.html). Treats the four-noble-truths-based supramundane progression, the *sapta-manaskāra* applied to the supramundane, *bhāvanā-svabhāva*, *karma*, *prakāra-bheda*, the attainment of arhatship, and *parinirvāṇa*. Ends with the colophon of the Śrāvakabhūmi as a whole.
