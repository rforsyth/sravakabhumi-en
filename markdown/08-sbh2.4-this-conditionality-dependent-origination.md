# This-Conditionality Dependent Origination

*Śrāvakabhūmi · sbh2.4*

What is this-conditionality dependent origination?

In the three times, it is: mere formation, mere dharma, mere entity, mere cause, mere effect — consistent with reasoning, namely, by dependence-reasoning, by cause-and-effect reasoning, and by proof-establishing reasoning. [Lacuna] It is the bringing forth of dharmas by dharmas alone, and the absence of an agent or experiencer. This is called the this-conditionality-dependent-origination object.

Attending to this object, the person of delusion-conduct in whom delusion predominates abandons delusion, thins it, and purifies the mind from delusion-conduct.
