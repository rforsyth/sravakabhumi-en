# Analysis of Elements

*Śrāvakabhūmi · sbh2.5*

What is the analysis of elements?

Namely, the six elements: the earth element, water element, fire element, wind element, space element, and consciousness element.

The earth element is twofold: inward and outward. The inward earth element: whatever in this body, internally, individually, is hard or harsh, has come to be taken up. The outward earth element: whatever externally is hard, has gone to hardness, has not come to be taken up.

What, further, is the inward earth element?

Hair of the head, body hair, nails, teeth, dust, dirt, skin, flesh, bone, sinew, vein, kidneys, heart, spleen, lungs, intestines, mesentery; stomach, bowel, liver, feces. This is called the inward earth element.

What, further, is the outward earth element?

Wood, clods, gravel, potsherds, trees, mountain-peaks — and anything else of this kind. This is called the outward earth element.

What is the water element?

The water element is twofold: inward and outward.

What is the inward water element?

Whatever, internally, individually, is moist or has gone to moistness, is water, has gone to the water element, has come to be taken up. Tears, sweat, saliva, mucus, fat, lymph, marrow, grease, bile, phlegm, pus, blood, brain, brain-membrane, urine. This is called the inward water element.

What is the outward water element?

Whatever externally is water, has gone to water, is moist, has gone to moistness, has not come to be taken up. Springs, lakes, ponds, rivers, fountains — and anything else of this kind. This is called the outward water element.

What is the fire element?

The fire element is twofold: inward and outward.

What is the inward fire element?

Whatever, internally, individually, is fire or heat, has gone to fire or heat, has come to be taken up. Namely: the fire in this body by which the body is heated, fevered, burned through; by which what is eaten, drunk, chewed, and tasted goes to proper digestion with ease; and through whose excess one comes to be reckoned [Conjectural: ‘feverish’].

What is the outward fire element?

Whatever externally is fire or heat, has gone to fire or heat, has not come to be taken up. That which people seek from fire-sticks and cow-dung powder; that which, having arisen, burns even a village, even part of a village, a city, part of a city, a country, part of a country, an island, a thicket, a forest, wood, grass, or cow-dung — burning these, it goes on. And anything else of this kind. [Lacuna]

What is the wind element?

The wind element is twofold: inward and outward. The inward wind element: whatever, internally, individually, is wind or has gone to wind — that which is lightness, motion, come to be taken up. Furthermore, in this body there are: upward-going winds, downward-going winds, side-resting winds; belly-resting winds; back-resting winds; wind-swelling winds; knife winds, *pippalaka* winds, blade winds; the in-and-out breaths; winds following the limbs and minor limbs.

What is the outward wind element?

Whatever externally is wind or has gone to wind — that lightness and motion, not come to be taken up. There are externally: eastern winds, southern winds, northern winds, western winds; dust-bearing winds, dustless winds, small winds, great winds, *viśva* winds, *vairambha* winds, wind-circle winds. There is a time when a great mass of wind has accumulated and topples even tree-tops, even wall-tops, even mountain-peaks; having toppled them, with nothing to sustain it, it dies away. There are beings who seek [wind] with a corner of the robe, a palm-leaf, or a fan. Such wind, and anything else of this kind. [Lacuna]

What is the space element?

The cavities of the eye, ear, nose, mouth, and throat; that by which one ingests, that into which one ingests, that through which what is ingested passes; that through which it flows out below; and anything else of this kind. This is called the space element.

What is the consciousness element?

Eye-consciousness; ear-, nose-, tongue-, body-, mind-consciousness; That, further, is mind, [Conjectural: mentality, and consciousness]. This is called the consciousness element.

The conceit-conduct person, attending to this analysis of elements, dispels the perception of the body as a unified lump. He also obtains the perception of unattractiveness; and no longer becomes elevated [proud] through [the body]. He attenuates the conceit and purifies the mind from conceit-conduct. This is called the analysis of elements — the conduct-purifying object for the conceit-conduct person.
